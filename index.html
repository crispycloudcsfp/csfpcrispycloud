About Crispy Cloud

A little crunch.
A whole lot of cloud.

Crispy Cloud is a local snack brand in Barangay Dolores, City of San Fernando, Pampanga, serving Japanese-inspired deep-fried cream sandwiches. We bring together a golden, crispy outer layer and a smooth, flavorful cream filling for a unique dessert experience.

With seven flavors—Crème Brûlée, Strawberry Cream, Matcha Cream, Yema Cream, Chocolate Cream, Ube Cream, and Mango Cream—we aim to offer a fun, delicious, and affordable snack for dessert lovers.

Our goal is simple: serve a memorable bite that combines crisp texture, creamy filling, and exciting flavors.
